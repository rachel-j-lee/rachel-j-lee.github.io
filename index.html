A little about
me.

I'm a second year student at the University of Toronto double majoring in Cognitive Science and Statistics.

I'm relatively new to both fields, as I took my first cognitive science and statistics courses during my first year at UofT. Since then, I've been trying to explore different pathways in both fields and find opportunities to apply my coursework to personal interests and hobbies. I'm someone who enjoys exploring new ideas, taking on meaningful challenges, and finding opportunities to grow both personally and professionally.

Outside of school, I enjoy reading all kinds of books, learning how to crochet, and watching sports! I'm continuously looking for new ways to combine creativity, curiosity, and dedication in everything I do.

This website is a little collection of my journey so far: the experiences I've gained, projects I've worked on, and achievements I'm proud of.
